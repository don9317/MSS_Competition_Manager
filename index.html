Playing Surfaces

Add each physical court once. If a court is available in more than one time block, add the same court name again with the additional hours; MSS will combine those windows automatically and keep the court list sorted.

Date-Specific Court Availability

Block a court for one playing date, or limit it to a special availability window. These exceptions are hard scheduling constraints.

Check every court that should receive this exception. You can block several courts for Week 1 in one step.

Court Groups

Court groups are reusable sets of playing surfaces. Multiple divisions can use the same group at different times. A group named “All Courts” automatically includes every current playing surface.

When divisions exist, choose the division that will use this court group. The assignment is saved automatically into Division Scheduling. The same court group can still be assigned to additional divisions later.

League Scheduling Rules

These rules are used by the schedule generator and conflict checker. Toggle the basic policies on or off for this league.

Allow Back-to-Back GamesYES permits a team to play again in the immediately following time slot.
Play Every Pool Opponent Before RepeatsPrioritizes playing all available teams at least once before repeat matchups.
Avoid Immediate RematchesA team’s next chronological game may not be against the same opponent as its previous game, regardless of elapsed time or date.
Protect Shared CoachesPrevents teams with the same coach from being scheduled at the same time.
Honor Team Schedule RequestsUses imported restrictions such as no Sunday or no morning games.
Keep Teams in Assigned PoolLeague games are generated within the approved pool unless this rule is changed in a future advanced format.
Always enforced: no team can play itself; a court cannot host two games at the same time; a team cannot be in two games at the same time.
