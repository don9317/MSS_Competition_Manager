Court Groups

Create reusable groups of surfaces. A division is assigned to one court group and returns to those surfaces each week. A group named “All Courts” automatically includes every current playing surface.

Division Court & Time Assignments

Assign every division to a repeating Sunday time window and court group. These assignments remain the same each week.

Matchup rules: no self-games, no immediate rematches, and all pool opponents are prioritized before repeats.
